Through _Google for Nonprofits_ program, we have free Google Workspace subscriptions. This means that we can offer the community the full Google business tools, and set up distribution lists for emails.

## Google Workspace

Anyone that runs a community group needs to be able to communicate by email, work on and share documents, and host meetings. Having access to a Google Workspace account will give you the full range of tools needed to conduct community work.

## Google Group

We can offer a Google group, which can act as the email address for your community group. You do not need to have an account in the Cloud Native Norway to get email through this email, or to be an administrator of it. If your group only wants an email and not the rest of the Google tools, you can get one by filling out [this](https://forms.gle/dy1p9iubA81eiSXG8) form. If you want to use your own domain with a group, please see the section on getting adding domains to our Google Workspace.
